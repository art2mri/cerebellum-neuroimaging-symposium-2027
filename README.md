# Cerebellum Neuroimaging Symposium 2027

Official website repository for the **Cerebellum Neuroimaging Symposium 2027**, an international scientific meeting focused on cerebellar imaging, clinical translation, and trial-ready biomarkers.

## Live website

**https://art2mri.github.io/cerebellum-neuroimaging-symposium-2027/**

## Registration

Registration is open through Humanitix:

**https://events.humanitix.com/cerebellum-neuroimaging-symposium-2027**

## Abstract submission

Abstract submissions are open until **January 15, 2027**:

**https://art2mri.github.io/cerebellum-neuroimaging-symposium-2027/abstract-submission.html**

The public submission form is hosted as a Google Apps Script web app administered through the symposium Google Drive. It follows the symposium template: presenting-author details, oral/poster preference, scientific topic, title and authors, a structured abstract of up to 500 words, and up to two optional figures. Respondents do not need a Google or Gmail account to submit or upload figures.
